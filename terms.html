What the app does

You photograph a page of a restaurant menu, or pick a photo of one. The app sends it to our server and to Google’s Gemini AI, which read the dishes on it; the app then lists them as printed, with a translation into your language, a short description, the usual ingredients and allergens, and a picture. The picture is a photo from our library or a partner site (tap ⓘ on it to see the source), or an illustration drawn by AI where there is no photo — it is marked as such. The bundled example menu shows all of this without a menu at hand.

AI results can be wrong

Everything the app shows about a dish is generated or matched automatically and can be incomplete or wrong: the reading of the menu, the translation, the description, the ingredients, the allergens and the picture.

  • Ingredients and allergens describe a typical recipe, not how this restaurant prepares the dish. “No common allergens” means none was found in a typical recipe, not that the dish is safe for you. The app is not medical, dietary or allergy advice. If you have an allergy, an intolerance or a diet, always ask the staff before you order.
  • An AI illustration shows how a dish usually looks. The plate you are served may look different.
  • Prices, availability and the restaurant’s own descriptions are the restaurant’s business; the app does not check them.

If something is offensive or clearly wrong, use the “Report” link at the foot of the dish card, or write to us.
