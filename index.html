Dish Eye Privacy Policy

Developer: Volodymyr Budko (EyeAppies)
Contact: eyeappies@protonmail.com
Effective date: October 3, 2026

In short. Dish Eye has no accounts, no advertising and no behavioral analytics. To read a menu, the app sends the photo you take to our server and to Google's Gemini service; the photo is not stored. The app knows an installation by a technical identifier, never by your name, email or phone number. We do not sell data or share it for advertising.

This policy covers the Dish Eye app for Android and iOS ("the app"), published under the name EyeAppies by Volodymyr Budko, an individual developer ("we"), who is responsible for the data described here. It explains what the app sends, why, and what happens to it.

What the app sends and why

The menu photo. When you scan a page, the photo is sent over an encrypted connection to our server and from there to Google's Gemini API, which reads the dishes on it. Before sending, the app re-encodes the photo without its metadata (such as where it was taken). Our server does not save the photo. Google processes it under the Gemini API terms for paid services: it does not use the photo to improve its products and keeps requests only for a limited time to detect abuse. What was read from the page — dish names, their descriptions, the menu's headings — is kept under a fingerprint of the photo (a SHA-256 hash), so that the next person who photographs the same menu gets the result at once. The fingerprint cannot be turned back into the photo, and nothing about who took the photo is kept with it. The app is meant for menus: please do not photograph people or personal documents with it.

An installation identifier. The app sends an identifier of this installation with its requests: on iPhone, a random code the app creates and keeps in the iOS Keychain; on Android, the identifier Android gives this app on this device (Android ID, different for every app). It is not linked to your name, email, phone number or any account. We use it to:

  • keep daily usage limits per installation (these counters are deleted after two days);
  • count the free pages an installation has read, when the app offers free pages before a subscription;
  • check whether the installation has an active subscription (the answer is remembered for up to one hour);
  • confirm that a request belongs to a session the app opened (a signed token valid for 24 hours, not stored on our server).

On both systems the identifier normally survives reinstalling the app, so that a reinstall does not reset the free pages. It may change after the device is reset or, on Android, if the app's signing key changes.

Your approximate country. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, determines from the IP address the country a request comes from. We use the country only to describe dishes as they are usually served there. The app does not use precise location and does not ask for location permission.

The app's language. The language you chose in the app is sent so that the dishes are explained in it.

Proof that the request comes from the genuine app. The app uses Firebase App Check (Google), which asks Apple (App Attest) or Google (Play Integrity) to confirm that the request comes from an unmodified copy of the app on a genuine device. During this check Firebase receives an integrity token and basic technical information (such as the device type and software versions); our server receives only a short-lived token saying the check passed.

Purchases. If you subscribe, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play or the App Store; we never see your payment details. Subscriptions are managed through RevenueCat, which receives this installation's purchase history, its identifier (described above) and technical information such as locale and currency. RevenueCat uses this to keep track of subscriptions and to give us aggregate subscription statistics (such as how many installations subscribe). Our server asks RevenueCat only whether the installation has an active subscription.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you tap to take a photo. To pick an existing photo, the app opens the system photo picker and receives only the photo you choose; it has no other access to your photo library.

What stays on your phone

Saved dishes, your language choice, the interface texts and the number of free pages left are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them; the installation identifier is the exception described above.

What the app does not do

No accounts or sign-in. No advertising, no advertising identifier, no tracking across other companies' apps or websites. No advertising or behavioral analytics SDKs. No crash-reporting SDKs. No access to contacts, precise location, the microphone or files. We do not sell or rent data, and we do not share it for advertising.

Service providers

They process data on our behalf and only for the purposes above:

  • Cloudflare — hosting of our server and its storage. Its request logs, which may include the IP address and the installation identifier, are kept for up to 7 days to run and protect the service.
  • Google — Gemini API (reading the photo), Firebase App Check and Play Integrity (app integrity), Google Play (distribution and payments).
  • Apple — App Attest (app integrity), App Store (distribution and payments).
  • RevenueCat — subscription status and subscription statistics.

These providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States.

Your rights and choices

You can use the app without the camera: pick a photo from your gallery or try the example menu included in the app. You can delete everything the app stores on your device by deleting the app.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to object to its processing, and to complain to a data protection authority. We process data to provide the service you ask for and, for usage limits and integrity checks, on our legitimate interest in protecting the service from abuse. Since the app has no accounts, we cannot look up data by your name or email. To have a subscription record deleted, write to us with the order number from your Google Play or App Store receipt. For any other question or request, write to eyeappies@protonmail.com.

Security

All traffic between the app and our server is encrypted (HTTPS). Sessions are signed by the server, and the keys to the services we use never leave it.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has sent us data, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new effective date.
